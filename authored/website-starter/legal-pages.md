# Legal pages: what each one must cover

These are outlines, not legal text. Write the pages from them using only facts found in the brief or
on the running site, and leave `[REPLACE: …]` wherever a fact is missing. The owner reviews before
launch, and a lawyer reviews anything regulated.

## Privacy policy

A visitor should be able to answer six questions from this page alone: who holds my data, what do
they have, why, who else sees it, how long is it kept, and how do I get it deleted.

| # | Section | Must say |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Who we are | The legal or trading name, the address if the business uses one, a contact email, and a data-protection contact if there is one. |
| 2 | What we collect | A table built from the real site: each form field, the newsletter address, analytics events, cookies and server logs, with where each comes from and whether it is required. |
| 3 | Why we use it | One purpose per data type. Where GDPR applies, the lawful basis for each: contract, consent, legitimate interest or legal obligation. |
| 4 | Cookies | The categories the site uses (necessary, analytics, marketing), a link to the Cookie policy, and how to change the choice. |
| 5 | Who we share it with | Every processor by name and role: hosting, email, analytics, forms, chat, payments; where each is; a link to each one's own policy. Say whether data is sold, and only "we do not sell" if that is true. |
| 6 | Where it is stored | The countries, and any transfer outside the visitor's region with the safeguard used. |
| 7 | How long we keep it | A retention period per data type, or the rule used to decide it. |
| 8 | Your rights | Access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawing consent, and complaining to a regulator. How to ask, and how long an answer takes. |
| 9 | Children | Whether the site is for adults only, or the minimum age and how consent is obtained. |
| 10 | Security | The measures in place, described plainly. |
| 11 | Changes and date | How changes are announced, and the date of the last update. |

## Terms and conditions

| # | Section | Must say |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Who we are | The entity's name and how to contact it. |
| 2 | What the site offers | The service in scope, and what is not included. |
| 3 | Acceptable use | What is not allowed: unlawful content, interfering with the site, scraping where it is not permitted, impersonation. |
| 4 | Accounts | If there are accounts: accurate details at sign-up, one person per account, care of passwords, grounds for suspension. |
| 5 | Prices, payment, refunds | If the site sells: currency, taxes, renewals, cancellation and refund terms. |
| 6 | Our content | Who owns the text, images, logos and code, and the licence visitors receive (personal, non-commercial). A trademark note where relevant. |
| 7 | Content visitors submit | If visitors can post or upload: the licence they grant, moderation, removal, and who is responsible. |
| 8 | Third-party links and services | That the site is not responsible for them, and that their own terms apply. |
| 9 | Disclaimers | That the information is provided as it stands, and is not professional advice unless it is. |
| 10 | Limitation of liability | What liability is excluded or capped, as far as the law allows. |
| 11 | Suspension and termination | The grounds, the notice given, and what happens to the visitor's data and content. |
| 12 | Governing law and disputes | Which law applies and where disputes are heard, with any required complaint or mediation step first. |
| 13 | Changes | How updated terms are announced, and when they take effect. |
| 14 | Date | Last updated. |

## Cookie policy

Needed when the site sets non-essential cookies or uses local storage for analytics or marketing.

- One paragraph on what cookies are.
- A table with every cookie: name, who sets it, purpose, type (necessary, analytics, marketing), lifetime.
- How to control them: the consent banner and the browser's own settings.

Build the table from the site, not from a template. Every name in it is a cookie the site really sets:
check in a private window with the developer tools open.

## Cookie consent banner

- It appears before any non-essential cookie or third-party script runs.
- Reject is as easy as Accept, on the first screen.
- Nothing is pre-ticked, and the site stays usable after Reject.
- It links to the privacy policy and the cookie policy.
- It remembers the choice and offers a way to change it later.

## Rules for an agent writing these pages

1. Use only facts from the brief and from the site itself: read its forms, its scripts and the
   cookies it sets.
2. Never invent an entity name, registration number, address, retention period or governing law.
   Write `[REPLACE: …]` instead.
3. Never claim a certification, an insurance or a "compliance" the owner has not stated.
4. "Last updated" is the launch date.
5. Hand back every placeholder and every claim for the owner to confirm. Laws differ by country and
   by sector, and a regulated business (finance, health, children's data) needs a lawyer's review.
